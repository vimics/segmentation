Vimics Bone Viewer

What is stored, and why

The 3D model is built in your browser. Separately, when you agree on the start page, Vimics stores a de-identified copy of the scan to develop bone segmentation and labelling software.

What Vimics stores

  • The CT images, with their geometry and scanner settings
  • The details you enter: patient name, cat or dog, breed, age, weight and clinician name
  • The study date, the threshold in use, the app version, and the time you agreed

What is removed before anything leaves your computer

Your browser keeps only an approved list of technical DICOM fields. Everything else is dropped, including:

  • Owner and responsible person names
  • The patient name and ID recorded in the file
  • Clinic name and address, and referring or performing clinician names
  • Free-text notes and descriptions, private vendor fields and nested data
  • Original study and series identifiers, which are replaced with random ones

Series marked as having text burned into the images are not stored at all. The original files you selected are never sent.

Where it goes

Data is stored in Vimics' Microsoft SharePoint storage, for the development work described above.

Your choices

The viewer shows a case reference once a scan is stored. To have a case deleted, send that reference to info@vimics.vet. Data already used to train a model cannot be removed from that trained model.

Please agree only if you are entitled to share the scan, including where your clinic has obligations to the animal's owner.
